Citizenship English test: reading and writing vocabulary

The English part of the naturalization test has three skills: speaking (judged during your interview), reading and writing. For reading and writing the officer uses sentences built only from the words below, published by USCIS.

In the interview you read aloud up to three sentences and need to read one correctly, and you write up to three sentences and need to write one correctly. Capital letters and punctuation are not graded; you must spell words so the officer can read them.
