What is the “rule of law”?

US citizenship 2008 civics test, question 12 of 100

Answer

Any one of these is a correct answer:

Everyone must follow the law.
Leaders must obey the law.
Government must obey the law.
No one is above the law.

On the 2025 test

This question is also on the 2025 test (question 13). Accepted answers there: Everyone must follow the law.; Leaders must obey the law.; Government must obey the law.; No one is above the law.. Open it.

About this question

The officer asks the civics test out loud and you answer out loud. On the 2008 civics test the officer asks up to 10 questions and you need 6 correct. You may give any accepted answer, and USCIS says other correct answers may exist; use the ones listed.
