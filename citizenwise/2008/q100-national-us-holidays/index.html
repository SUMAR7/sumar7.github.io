Name two national U.S. holidays.

US citizenship 2008 civics test, question 100 of 100

Answer

Any one of these is a correct answer:

New Year’s Day
Martin Luther King, Jr. Day
Presidents’ Day
Memorial Day
Juneteenth
Independence Day
Labor Day
Columbus Day
Veterans Day
Thanksgiving
Christmas *  If you are 65 years old or older and have been a legal permanent resident of the United States for 20 or more years, you

The officer wants two answers. Give two from this list.

About this question

The officer asks the civics test out loud and you answer out loud. On the 2008 civics test the officer asks up to 10 questions and you need 6 correct. You may give any accepted answer, and USCIS says other correct answers may exist; use the ones listed.
