Who is the Chief Justice of the United States now?

US citizenship 2008 civics test, question 40 of 100

Answer

John G. Roberts, Jr.
That was the answer on 28 Nov 2026. USCIS wants the name of the official serving at your interview. Check uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates before you go.

On the 2025 test

This question is also on the 2025 test (question 57). Its answer depends on the day or place, as above. Open it.

About this question

The officer asks the civics test out loud and you answer out loud. On the 2008 civics test the officer asks up to 10 questions and you need 6 correct. You may give any accepted answer, and USCIS says other correct answers may exist; use the ones listed.
