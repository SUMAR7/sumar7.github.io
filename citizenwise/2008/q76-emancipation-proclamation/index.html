What did the Emancipation Proclamation do?

US citizenship 2008 civics test, question 76 of 100

Answer

Any one of these is a correct answer:

freed the slaves
freed slaves in the Confederacy
freed slaves in the Confederate states
freed slaves in most Southern states

On the 2025 test

This question is also on the 2025 test (question 95). Accepted answers there: Freed the slaves; Freed slaves in the Confederacy; Freed slaves in the Confederate states; Freed slaves in most Southern states. Open it.

About this question

The officer asks the civics test out loud and you answer out loud. On the 2008 civics test the officer asks up to 10 questions and you need 6 correct. You may give any accepted answer, and USCIS says other correct answers may exist; use the ones listed.
