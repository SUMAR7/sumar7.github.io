Name one war fought by the United States in the 1900s.

US citizenship 2008 civics test, question 78 of 100 · ⭐ 65/20 question

Answer

Any one of these is a correct answer:

World War I
World War II
Korean War
Vietnam War
Persian Gulf War

On the 2025 test

This question is also on the 2025 test (question 100). Accepted answers there: World War I; World War II; Korean War; Vietnam War; Persian Gulf War. Open it.

About this question

The officer asks the civics test out loud and you answer out loud. On the 2008 civics test the officer asks up to 10 questions and you need 6 correct. If you are 65 or older and have been a permanent resident for 20 years or more, this is one of the 20 questions you may study. You may give any accepted answer, and USCIS says other correct answers may exist; use the ones listed.
