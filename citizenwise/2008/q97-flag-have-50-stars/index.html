Why does the flag have 50 stars?

US citizenship 2008 civics test, question 97 of 100 · ⭐ 65/20 question

Answer

Any one of these is a correct answer:

because there is one star for each state
because each star represents a state
because there are 50 states

On the 2025 test

This question is also on the 2025 test (question 122). Accepted answers there: Because there is one star for each state; Because each star represents a state; Because there are 50 states. Open it.

About this question

The officer asks the civics test out loud and you answer out loud. On the 2008 civics test the officer asks up to 10 questions and you need 6 correct. If you are 65 or older and have been a permanent resident for 20 years or more, this is one of the 20 questions you may study. You may give any accepted answer, and USCIS says other correct answers may exist; use the ones listed.
