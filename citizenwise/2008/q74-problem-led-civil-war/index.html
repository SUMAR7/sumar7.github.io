Name one problem that led to the Civil War.

US citizenship 2008 civics test, question 74 of 100

Answer

Any one of these is a correct answer:

slavery
economic reasons
states’ rights

About this question

The officer asks the civics test out loud and you answer out loud. On the 2008 civics test the officer asks up to 10 questions and you need 6 correct. You may give any accepted answer, and USCIS says other correct answers may exist; use the ones listed.
