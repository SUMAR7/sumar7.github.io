Citizenship interview: what to expect

A short plan of the naturalization interview so nothing surprises you.

  1. Oath to tell the truth. The officer swears you in to tell the truth.
  2. Questions about your application. The officer goes through your Form N-400 and asks about you. Your spoken English is judged here.
  3. English reading and writing. You read and write sentences. See the vocabulary.
  4. Civics test. The officer asks up to 20 questions (2025 test) or 10 (2008 test) out loud. Which test applies?

Good to know

  • The civics test is oral. Practise saying your answers out loud, not only reading them.
  • You can give any accepted answer. You do not need to remember all of them.
  • Some answers change with elections: answer with the person holding the office on the day of your interview.
  • Applicants 65+ with 20+ years as permanent residents study the 20 starred questions.

Rules change. Always follow your interview notice and uscis.gov/citizenship.
