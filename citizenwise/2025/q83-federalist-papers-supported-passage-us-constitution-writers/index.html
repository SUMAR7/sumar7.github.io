CitizenWise › 2025 test › Colonial Period and Independence › Question 83

The Federalist Papers supported the passage of the U.S. Constitution. Name one of the writers.

US citizenship 2025 civics test, question 83 of 128

Answer

Any one of these is a correct answer:

James Madison
Alexander Hamilton
John Jay
Publius

On the 2008 test

This question is also on the 2008 test (question 67). Accepted answers there: James Madison; Alexander Hamilton; John Jay; Publius. Open it.

About this question

The officer asks the civics test out loud and you answer out loud. On the 2025 civics test the officer asks up to 20 questions and you need 12 correct. You may give any accepted answer, and USCIS says other correct answers may exist; use the ones listed.
