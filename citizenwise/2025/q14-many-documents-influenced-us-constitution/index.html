Many documents influenced the U.S. Constitution. Name one.

US citizenship 2025 civics test, question 14 of 128

Answer

Any one of these is a correct answer:

Declaration of Independence
Articles of Confederation
Federalist Papers
Anti-Federalist Papers
Virginia Declaration of Rights
Fundamental Orders of Connecticut
Mayflower Compact
Iroquois Great Law of Peace

About this question

The officer asks the civics test out loud and you answer out loud. On the 2025 civics test the officer asks up to 20 questions and you need 12 correct. You may give any accepted answer, and USCIS says other correct answers may exist; use the ones listed.
