Name two promises that new citizens make in the Oath of Allegiance.

US citizenship 2025 civics test, question 67 of 128

Answer

Any one of these is a correct answer:

Give up loyalty to other countries
Defend the U.S. Constitution
Obey the laws of the United States
Serve in the military if needed
Serve help, do important work for the nation if needed
Be loyal to the United States

The officer wants two answers. Give two from this list.

About this question

The officer asks the civics test out loud and you answer out loud. On the 2025 civics test the officer asks up to 20 questions and you need 12 correct. You may give any accepted answer, and USCIS says other correct answers may exist; use the ones listed.
