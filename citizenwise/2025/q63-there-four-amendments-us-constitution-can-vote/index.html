There are four amendments to the U.S. Constitution about who can vote. Describe one of them.

US citizenship 2025 civics test, question 63 of 128

Answer

Any one of these is a correct answer:

Citizens eighteen 18 and older can vote.
You don’t have to pay a poll tax to vote.
Any citizen can vote. Women and men can vote.
A male citizen of any race can vote.

About this question

The officer asks the civics test out loud and you answer out loud. On the 2025 civics test the officer asks up to 20 questions and you need 12 correct. You may give any accepted answer, and USCIS says other correct answers may exist; use the ones listed.
