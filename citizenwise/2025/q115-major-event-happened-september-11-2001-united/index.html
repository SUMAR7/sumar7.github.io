What major event happened on September 11, 2001 in the United States?

US citizenship 2025 civics test, question 115 of 128 · ⭐ 65/20 question

Answer

Any one of these is a correct answer:

Terrorists attacked the United States
Terrorists took over two planes and crashed them into the World Trade Center in New York City
Terrorists took over a plane and crashed into the Pentagon in Arlington, Virginia
Terrorists took over a plane originally aimed at Washington, D.C., and crashed in a field in Pennsylvania

On the 2008 test

This question is also on the 2008 test (question 86). Accepted answers there: Terrorists attacked the United States.. Open it.

About this question

The officer asks the civics test out loud and you answer out loud. On the 2025 civics test the officer asks up to 20 questions and you need 12 correct. If you are 65 or older and have been a permanent resident for 20 years or more, this is one of the 20 questions you may study. You may give any accepted answer, and USCIS says other correct answers may exist; use the ones listed.
