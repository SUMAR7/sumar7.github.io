Why is the Declaration of Independence important?

US citizenship 2025 civics test, question 8 of 128

Answer

Any one of these is a correct answer:

It says America is free from British control.
It says all people are created equal.
It identifies inherent rights.
It identifies individual freedoms.

About this question

The officer asks the civics test out loud and you answer out loud. On the 2025 civics test the officer asks up to 20 questions and you need 12 correct. You may give any accepted answer, and USCIS says other correct answers may exist; use the ones listed.
