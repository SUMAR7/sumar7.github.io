Why does the flag have 13 stripes?

US citizenship 2025 civics test, question 121 of 128 · ⭐ 65/20 question

Answer

Any one of these is a correct answer:

Because there were 13 original colonies
Because the stripes represent the original colonies

On the 2008 test

This question is also on the 2008 test (question 96). Accepted answers there: because there were 13 original colonies; because the stripes represent the original colonies. Open it.

About this question

The officer asks the civics test out loud and you answer out loud. On the 2025 civics test the officer asks up to 20 questions and you need 12 correct. If you are 65 or older and have been a permanent resident for 20 years or more, this is one of the 20 questions you may study. You may give any accepted answer, and USCIS says other correct answers may exist; use the ones listed.
