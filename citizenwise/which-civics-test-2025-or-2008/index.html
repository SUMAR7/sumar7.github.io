Which civics test will I take: the 2025 or the 2008 version?

It depends on when you filed your naturalization application (Form N-400).

2025 test

Applications filed on or after 20 October 2025.

  • 128 questions in the study list
  • The officer asks up to 20
  • You need 12 correct

All 128 questions

2008 test

Applications filed before 20 October 2025.

  • 100 questions in the study list
  • The officer asks up to 10
  • You need 6 correct

All 100 questions

Both tests are oral: the officer asks, you answer out loud. The pass mark is 60% in both. Your interview notice and USCIS tell you which version applies to you. If in doubt, study the 2025 list: many questions are on both.

Applicants 65+ with 20+ years as permanent residents study 20 starred questions on either test.
