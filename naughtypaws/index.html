How to play

  1. Drag anywhere to walk. Push gently to sneak, push further to run.
  2. Follow the paw prints to your next naughty task. Simple ones start by themselves; for the big ones, tap the action button to jump up, swat, grab or unroll.
  3. Stay out of the yellow cone. That is what Emma can see. Crashes are loud: she comes to look, so run and hide until she gives up.
  4. Finish every task to win. Get caught three times and the level is over; fewer catches mean more stars.
