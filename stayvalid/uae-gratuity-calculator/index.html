How UAE gratuity is calculated

  • You need at least one year of continuous service. Under one year there is no gratuity.
  • 21 days of basic salary for each of the first 5 years, and 30 days for each year after that. Part years count in proportion.
  • One day of basic salary = monthly basic ÷ 30, and the law uses your last basic salary for every year.
  • Only the basic salary counts: not housing, transport or any other allowance.
  • Unpaid leave and unpaid absence days are taken off your service.
  • The total is capped at two years’ wage. Most guides read that as 24 × basic salary; the cap only matters after about 25.5 years of service.
  • Part-time workers get gratuity in proportion to their contract hours.

Does resigning or dismissal reduce gratuity?

No. The 2021 law has no cut for resigning, and gratuity is not lost on dismissal, even summary dismissal under Article 44. The old 1980 law’s resignation cuts no longer apply.

Some old unlimited contracts converted under Article 68(3) may keep the old rules, so check your contract if yours is one of them.

When it must be paid

Within 14 days after the contract ends (Article 53). A claim is not heard after 2 years (Article 54(9), as amended by Decree-Law 9 of 2024). Deductions are allowed only for amounts you owe under the law or a court judgment (Article 51(7)).

Why your employer’s figure may differ

  • Some employers work out a day as basic × 12 ÷ 365, which gives about 1.4% less. This is the most common reason.
  • Counting the service in months instead of days can move the figure by a few dirhams.
  • After year 5: the official DDA free-zone calculator gives 21 days for the part year right after year 5. We follow the law text, Article 51(2)(b): 30 days for any service after 5 years.
  • Using the total salary instead of the basic salary.

DIFC and ADGM

  • DIFC: gratuity is paid only for service before 1 Feb 2020. From then the employer pays DEWS savings instead: 5.83% of monthly basic under 5 years of service, 8.33% from 5 years.
  • ADGM: 21 days a year for the first 5 years, 30 days after, on annual basic ÷ 365. No cap, and it is paid whatever the reason for leaving.
  • In both, the basic salary is taken as at least 50% of total pay.

Who this does not cover

Not for: UAE nationals (pension instead), domestic workers (separate law), government jobs.

If your employer joined the savings scheme (Cabinet Resolution 96 of 2023), gratuity covers your service up to the day before you were enrolled.

Questions people ask

How many days of gratuity do I get per year in the UAE?

21 days of basic salary for each of the first 5 years, and 30 days for each year after that. Part years count in proportion.

Is gratuity calculated on basic or total salary?

On the basic salary only, and on your last basic salary. Allowances are not included.

Do I get gratuity if I resign before 5 years?

Yes, as long as you have at least one year of continuous service. The 2021 law does not reduce gratuity for resigning.

Do I get gratuity for less than one year of service?

No. The law needs at least one year of continuous service (Article 51(2)).

When must my employer pay my gratuity?

Within 14 days after the contract ends (Article 53).

Is there a maximum gratuity?

Yes: two years’ wage (Article 51(6)). Most guides read this as 24 × your basic salary.
