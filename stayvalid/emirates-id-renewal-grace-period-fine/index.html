The rules

  • You can renew up to 6 months before the card expires.
  • After it expires there is a one-month grace period with no late fee.
  • After the grace period the late fee is AED 20 for each day.
  • The late fee stops at AED 1,000, which is reached after 50 days.

How the month is counted

A month runs to the same date in the next month, or to the last day of a shorter month: a card that expires on 31 January has its grace period end on 28 February (29 in a leap year).

Questions people ask

Is there a grace period after the Emirates ID expires?

Yes, one month. After that a late fee of AED 20 a day applies.

How much is the fine for an expired Emirates ID?

AED 20 for each day after the one-month grace period, up to a maximum of AED 1,000.

What is the maximum Emirates ID late fee?

AED 1,000.

How early can I renew my Emirates ID?

Up to 6 months before it expires.
