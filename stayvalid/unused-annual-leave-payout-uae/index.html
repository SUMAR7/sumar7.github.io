How much leave you earn

  • 30 days for each year of service.
  • Service over 6 months but under 1 year: 2 days for each completed month.
  • Part of the final year: 30 × days served in that leave year ÷ 365. A job that ends on the last day of a leave year earns that year’s full 30 days.
  • Service of 6 months or less: the law grants nothing explicit, so the payout may be 0. Check your contract.
  • Leave is counted in calendar days: rest days that fall inside your leave count as leave (Article 29(7)).

How the payout is worked out

  • Unused days × daily basic salary, where daily basic = monthly basic ÷ 30 (Executive Regulation Article 19(2); Article 67).
  • Unused leave is paid when you leave, however long it has built up (Article 29(9)).
  • Pay while you are on leave is different: that is your full salary (Article 29(6)).
  • Some employers use basic × 12 ÷ 365 for a day, about 1.4% less; the calculator shows that figure too.
  • For part of the final year some employers count 2.5 days per completed month; the calculator shows both when they differ.

Carrying leave forward

You can carry forward up to half of your annual leave, with your employer’s approval (Executive Regulation Article 19(1)).

Questions people ask

Is unused annual leave paid on basic or total salary in the UAE?

On the basic salary: unused days × monthly basic ÷ 30 (Executive Regulation Article 19(2)).

How many days of annual leave do I get in the UAE?

30 days for each year of service, or 2 days for each completed month when service is over 6 months but under 1 year.

Is unused leave paid if I resign?

Yes. Unused leave is paid when you leave, however long it has built up (Article 29(9)).

Can I carry annual leave forward to next year?

Up to half of your annual leave, with your employer’s approval (Executive Regulation Article 19(1)).
