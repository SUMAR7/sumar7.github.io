What goes into a final settlement

  • Salary not yet paid, up to your last working day, on your total salary.
  • Pay in lieu of notice, if the notice was not worked: total salary ÷ 30 × the notice days not served (Article 43).
  • Unused annual leave: unused days × basic salary ÷ 30 (Executive Regulation Article 19(2)).
  • End-of-service gratuity on your last basic salary (Article 51).
  • Any other amounts in your contract.

Notice works both ways

If the employer ends the contract and you do not work the notice, it pays you total salary ÷ 30 for each notice day not served. If you resign and leave without working your notice, the calculator shows that amount as owed by you (Article 43).

When it must be paid

Within 14 days after your last day (Article 53). A claim is not heard after 2 years (Article 54(9)).

Your ticket home

The employer pays the cost of going home, unless you joined another employer or the contract ended because of you (Article 13(12)). The calculator does not put a figure on this.

Check your employer’s figures

Already have your settlement paper? Use “Check my settlement”: enter the employer’s figure for each line and it names the likely reason for any difference. The usual reasons are allowances used where only the basic salary counts, a daily rate of basic × 12 ÷ 365, counting in months, and 21 instead of 30 days a year after year 5.

Questions people ask

What is included in a UAE final settlement?

Unpaid salary up to the last day, pay in lieu of notice if any, unused annual leave, the end-of-service gratuity, and any other amounts in the contract.

How long does my employer have to pay the final settlement?

Within 14 days after the contract ends (Article 53).

Is pay in lieu of notice worked out on basic or total salary?

On the total salary: the law says “last wage” (Article 43).

Is unused leave paid on basic or total salary?

On the basic salary (Executive Regulation Article 19(2)).

Does resigning reduce my gratuity?

No, not under the 2021 law.
