Overtime rates

  • Hourly basic = monthly basic ÷ 30 ÷ 8 (Article 67 and the 8-hour day in Article 17).
  • Ordinary overtime: hourly basic × 1.25 (Article 19(2)).
  • Overtime between 10 pm and 4 am: hourly basic × 1.50. Shift workers get × 1.25 instead (Article 19(3)).

Limits

  • At most 2 overtime hours a day, and no more than 144 hours of total work in 3 weeks (Article 19(1)).
  • Board members, supervisors with the employer’s powers, sea crews and continuous shifts are exempt from the hour limits (Executive Regulation Article 15(4)). It is unclear whether they also lose overtime pay.

Work on a rest day or public holiday

You get either another day off or cash (Articles 19(4) and 28(2)). The cash amount can be read two ways: one day’s total salary plus 50% of the daily basic, or 50% of the daily basic only. The calculator shows both and recommends the day off.

Ramadan

Normal working hours are 2 hours shorter for all private-sector workers during Ramadan (Executive Regulation Article 15(2)).

Questions people ask

How is overtime calculated in the UAE?

Monthly basic ÷ 30 ÷ 8 gives the hourly basic; ordinary overtime is paid at 1.25 times that (Article 19(2)).

What is the overtime rate at night?

1.5 times the hourly basic between 10 pm and 4 am, or 1.25 for shift workers (Article 19(3)).

How many overtime hours are allowed in the UAE?

At most 2 a day, and no more than 144 hours of total work in 3 weeks (Article 19(1)).

What do I get for working on a public holiday?

Another day off or cash (Article 28(2)). The law can be read two ways on the cash amount, so the calculator shows both.
