UAE Residence Visa Grace Period: What Applies and What to Check

There is no single grace period for every UAE residence visa: it depends on the visa type, so check yours on the official ICP or GDRFA pages. Before renewing, make sure the passport is valid long enough with the check below.

The grace period

After a visa expires or is cancelled you may stay for 30 to 180 days, depending on the visa type. Check yours with ICP or GDRFA.

Because the period depends on the visa type, we do not give one figure for everyone. The official pages below have the period for your visa.

Before you renew

  • Your sponsor (your employer, or the family member who sponsors you) renews it before it expires.
  • To renew a UAE residence visa, the passport must be valid for at least 6 more months.
  • Since 1 January 2025, health insurance is needed to issue or renew the residence visa of private-sector employees and domestic workers in every emirate.
  • Emirates ID: renewal can start up to 6 months before it expires; after expiry there is a one-month grace period, then a late fee of AED 20 a day, up to AED 1,000.
