Notice Period in UAE Labour Law: Probation, Resignation and Pay in Lieu

Your notice period is set in your contract, within the limits of the law. Enter your total salary and the notice days to see the pay in lieu of notice.

↗ Open it full screen

After probation

  • Between 30 and 90 days, as your contract says, and the same for both sides unless a different term is better for the worker (Article 43(1)–(2)).
  • Pay in lieu of notice = total salary ÷ 30 × the notice days not worked (Article 43(3)–(4)). The law says “last wage”, so allowances count.
  • Notice given while you are on leave starts the day after your scheduled return (Article 35).

During probation

  • The employer ends the contract: 14 days’ notice (Article 9(1)).
  • You move to another UAE employer: 1 month (Article 9(3)).
  • You leave the UAE: 14 days (Article 9(4)).
  • Leaving without this notice means you owe pay for the missing notice (Article 9(5)).
  • A foreign worker who leaves the UAE without this notice can be barred from a new work permit for one year (Article 9(6)); MOHRE can exempt some workers (Article 9(7)).

Questions people ask

What is the notice period under UAE labour law?

Between 30 and 90 days after probation, as the contract says (Article 43).

What is the notice period during probation?

14 days if the employer ends the contract, 1 month if you move to another UAE employer, and 14 days if you leave the UAE (Article 9).

How is pay in lieu of notice calculated?

Total salary ÷ 30 × the notice days not worked (Article 43(3)–(4)).

What happens if I leave without serving notice?

You owe pay for the notice you did not give. During probation, a foreign worker who leaves the UAE without notice can be barred from a new work permit for one year (Article 9(6)).
