Dabby's Color Studio

Swipe to paint, unlock colors and stickers, and fill your own gallery.

A cheerful coloring game for kids aged 3 to 8, hosted by Dabby the paint drop. Paint 24 hand-drawn pictures of animals, yummy treats, toys and people. The brush always stays inside the lines, so every picture turns out great.

  • Ages 3–8
  • Phone & tablet
  • Landscape
  • Plays offline
Coming soon to Google Play

A look inside

What's inside

🖌️ Stays inside the lines

Swipe to paint: color only goes where the finger goes, and never spills into the next shape.

🐾 24 pictures, 4 packs

Animal Friends, Yummy Treats, Things & Toys and People, unlocked by collecting stars.

⭐ Levels & 24 badges

Every finished picture earns stars and XP. Level ups unlock new palettes, patterns, glitter, rainbow fills and sticker sets.

👀 Color ideas

Not sure which colors to use? Peek at a colored version and tap its colors to paint with them.

🎁 Daily gift

A present every day and a Picture of the Day worth double XP.

🖼️ My Gallery

Every finished picture hangs in the kid's own gallery, ready to show off.

For parents

  • No accounts, no chat, and the game collects no personal information.
  • Ads are family-safe and non-personalized (Google AdMob, child-directed), never during the first three pictures and never while painting.
  • One optional purchase, "No Ads + All Pictures", sits behind a grown-ups check and is handled by Google Play.
  • Settings and purchases are behind a grown-ups question.
