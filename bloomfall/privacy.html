Bloomfall: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how the game Bloomfall ("the app"), published by SUMAR_TECH ("we"), handles information. It covers the app on Android phones and tablets, Chromebooks and Android TV, and the web version of the game.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. You don't need an account to play, and we don't ask for your name, email or phone number. Your game progress (levels reached, stars, your garden, seeds and boosts, Daily Drop and Skyfall bests, and your settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. We run no servers of our own that receive it.

Garden snapshots

When you tap Share in your garden, the app makes a picture of your garden on your device. It goes only where you choose to send it, through the app you pick, under that app's own terms.

Advertising

On phones, tablets and Chromebooks the app shows ads through Google AdMob: an occasional ad when you leave a results screen, and optional ads you can choose to watch to unlock a seed or a boost upgrade. The Android TV version and the web version show no ads, and the app shows no between-level ads once you own Remove Ads + Premium (optional reward ads stay available if you want them). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or a region with similar privacy laws, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy & ad choices. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Google → Ads.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase, Remove Ads + Premium, is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only learns whether your Google account owns it, so it can unlock it on your devices.

Permissions

The app uses internet access for ads and purchases, and vibration for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or precise location.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Ad and purchase traffic from the app travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
