Skyport Isles: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

This policy explains how the game Skyport Isles ("the app"), published by SUMAR_TECH ("we"), handles information. It covers the app on Android phones, tablets and Chromebooks.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. You don't need an account to play, and we don't ask for your name, email or phone number. Every flight is played on your device; there is no online multiplayer and no chat. Your progress (stars, coins, boosters, the liveries you own and your settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. We run no servers of our own that receive it, and the app contains no analytics of its own.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: an occasional full-screen ad when you leave a results screen, and optional ads you can choose to watch, for example to continue a flight, to double the coins from a flight, for a free booster or for free coins. Players who buy Remove Ads see no between-flight ads. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or a region with similar privacy laws, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. Where this applies, you can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy options. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only receives a confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made, so it can turn off the between-flight ads, and so the purchase can be restored if you reinstall.

Permissions

The app uses internet access for ads and purchases, and vibration for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, files or location.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Ad and purchase traffic from the app travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
