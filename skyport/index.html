Skyport Isles

Draw the path. Land the planes.

Colourful planes call in from every edge of the screen, and you're the control tower. Drag a line from each plane straight onto the runway of its colour and watch it touch down. As the sky gets busier you'll juggle jets, helicopters and seaplanes, steer clear of ash clouds and land on aircraft carriers that are sailing the whole time. Just don't let two planes meet.

How to play

  1. Watch the edges. A marker shows where the next plane will appear and which colour it is.
  2. Draw a path from the plane to the runway of the same colour. Come in straight, from the chevron end, and the line locks on.
  3. Keep them apart. Planes that get too close flash red; two that touch cost you a life. Lose all three and the flight is grounded.
  4. Land the goal. Bring in every plane on the boarding pass to finish the flight. Land them back to back for streaks, and finish with all lives for three stars.

What's inside

5 islands, 60 flights

Tropical Palm Cove, desert Sandstone Dunes, smoky Volcano Ridge, snowy Frost Harbor and autumn Fleet Command, each ending in a busy Rush finale.

A look and a song for every island

Each island has its own scenery, weather and music track, and its flights move from bright daylight to golden hour to sunset.

New aircraft as you go

Fast jets, helicopters that land on helipads from any direction, and seaplanes that touch down on water lanes.

Moving carriers

On Fleet Command the runway itself is sailing. Line up behind the deck and land on the move.

Boosters

Slow-Mo halves the speed of every aircraft for a few seconds, and Tower Assist lands a waiting plane for you.

Paint shop

Spend the coins you earn on liveries for your whole fleet: Candy Stripe, Retro Chrome, Midnight and Royal Gold.

Made for touch

Draw two paths at once on a tablet, feel every touchdown through haptics, and play in portrait or landscape, fully offline.

Free to play

Skyport Isles is free, with occasional ads between flights and optional ads you can choose to watch to continue after a crash, double your coins, or get a free booster. A one-time Remove Ads purchase turns off the between-flight ads for good; the optional reward videos stay available if you want them.
