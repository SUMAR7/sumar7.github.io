Kids Learning ABC & 123

Learn letters, words and counting with Ollie the owl.

A cartoon picture book for preschool and kindergarten. Each chapter teaches a few letters or numbers, then turns into workbook pages: draw a line from A to Apple, find every B, put three apples in the basket. Ollie reads everything aloud, so children who can't read yet can play on their own.

  • Ages 3–6
  • Android TV
  • Phone & tablet
  • TV remote
  • Plays offline

A look inside

What's inside

🔤 A to Z

Big and small letters with a picture word for each, plus more words to discover.

🔢 Counting to 10

Numbers, number words and ten-frames, with Ollie counting every apple, fish and star aloud.

✏️ Workbook pages

Draw a line to match, find the letter, which one starts with B, what comes next, how many, which side has more.

📺 Made for the remote

Arrows and OK are all it takes on Android TV, and touch works on phones and tablets.

🗣️ Read aloud

Every word and question is spoken, so pre-readers can play without help.

⭐ Stars and stickers

Every page earns stars and every chapter adds a sticker to the album.

For parents

  • No accounts, no chat, and the app collects no personal information.
  • Phones and tablets: every page is open. A family-safe, non-personalized ad may follow a finished chapter, always announced by an "Ad break" screen. One optional purchase removes all ads.
  • Android TV: no ads. Letters A to I and numbers 1 to 5 are open to try, and the same purchase opens the rest of both books. Purchases sit behind a grown-ups question and are handled by Google Play.
  • Progress stays on the device and works offline.
