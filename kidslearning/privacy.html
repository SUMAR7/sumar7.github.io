Kids Learning ABC & 123: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 November 2026

This policy explains how the learning app Kids Learning ABC & 123 ("the app"), published by SUMAR_TECH ("we"), handles information. The app is made for young children and their families and is designed to follow the Google Play Families policy and the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). It covers the app on Android TV, phones, tablets and Chromebooks.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no sharing with other players. The app never asks for a name, email, phone number, photo or location. Pages read, stars, stickers and settings are stored only on the device and are deleted when the app is uninstalled. We run no servers of our own that receive information from the app, and the app contains no analytics.

Advertising (phones and tablets only)

On phones and tablets every page is open, and the app may show an ad through Google AdMob when a child leaves a finished chapter: never during the first finished chapter, never in the first minute, at most one every few minutes, and never while a page is being played. Every ad is announced by a clearly labeled "Ad break" screen. The Android TV version shows no ads.

Every ad request is marked as directed to children and as coming from a user under the age of consent, with a maximum ad content rating of G. This means Google serves only non-personalized, family-appropriate ads: no interest-based advertising and no remarketing. The app does not use or send the device's advertising ID. To deliver ads and prevent fraud, Google may still process limited technical information such as the IP address, device type and the fact that an ad was shown or tapped, as described in Google's policy for child-directed requests and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Voice

Ollie's voice is made of recordings that ship inside the app. The app does not use the microphone and does not record or send anything a child says.

Purchases

One optional one-time purchase removes all ads on phones and tablets, and on Android TV opens the rest of both books (on TV, letters A to I and numbers 1 to 5 can be used without paying). It is placed behind a grown-ups question in the app and is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see payment details. The app only receives a confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made, so it can remove ads, open the pages and restore the purchase after a reinstall or on another device with the same Google account. Parents can require approval for purchases in the Google Play settings (Family Link or purchase authentication).

Permissions

The app uses internet access only for ads (phones and tablets) and so Google Play can process and restore the purchase. It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, files or location.

Parents' rights

Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing stored by us to review or delete. To remove everything the app saved on a device, uninstall it or use Grown-ups → Start over inside the app. If you have any question about your child's privacy, contact us and we will help.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
