Twistwise

Solve your cube. Then learn how.

Stuck with a scrambled cube? Show each side to the camera, or paint the colours in by hand, and Twistwise walks you through the solution one clear turn at a time on a 3D cube. When you want to do it without the app, it teaches you the method layer by layer, with the reason for every step.

How it works

  1. Enter your cube. Scan it with the camera, one side at a time, or tap the colours onto the 3D cube. The app checks it as you go and tells you which sticker to look at if something cannot be right.
  2. Pick a solve. Fastest solve gets it done in about 20 turns for a 3x3. Teach me solves the same cube the way a person does, layer by layer.
  3. Follow the arrows. Arrows on the cube show which layer turns and which way, with plain words such as "Right side up". Step forward, step back, or let it play at your speed.
  4. Learn it. Short lessons show each stage on a fresh example until the moves feel familiar.

What's inside

Four cube sizes

2x2 and 3x3 are free. 4x4 and 5x5 come with Twistwise Pro, solved in the stages people use: centres, edge pairs, parity, then finish like a 3x3.

Camera scan

Hold each side inside the square and capture it. Colours are read on your phone; pictures are never saved or sent anywhere.

3D, one turn at a time

Arrows on the cube, plain words, and half turns shown as two single turns so nothing goes by too fast.

Lessons

The 2x2 course is free in full and the 3x3 course is free up to the first layer. Each lesson uses a new example every time.

Play and timer

Swipe the stickers to solve a scrambled cube on your phone, with undo and hints. The timer gives scrambles, best time and average of 5.

Works offline

No account needed. Your cubes, times and settings stay on your device.

Free, with a fair upgrade

There is no ad when the app opens and none between you and your solution. You get one free solve every day; for another, or for a camera scan after your first, you can choose to watch one short ad. Twistwise Pro is a single payment, not a subscription: it adds the 4x4 and 5x5, the full 3x3 course, unlimited solves and scans, and removes every ad.
