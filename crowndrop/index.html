Crown Drop

Drop in. Loot up. Take the crown.

Twenty players jump from the plane. You pick where to land, grab the first gun you find and start looting crates and airdrops while the safe zone closes in. Throw grenades, pop a shield, level up with power-ups mid-match, and be the last one standing to wear the crown.

How to play

  1. Jump from the plane when it's over the spot you like, and steer your parachute down.
  2. Move with the left stick. Your hero aims and fires at the nearest rival on their own, so you can focus on positioning.
  3. Loot crates, fallen rivals and airdrops for better guns, armour, grenades and shields. Hold the grenade button to cook it, but don't hold it too long.
  4. Stay inside the circle. The zone shrinks in four phases, and the final circle brings airstrikes. Last one standing takes the crown.

What's inside

7 battlegrounds

Dustbowl Town, Fort Iron, Scrapyard Canyon, Greenwood Jungle, Midnight City, Frostpeak Pass and Mystic Grove unlock as your level rises.

8 weapons, 4 rarities

From the frying pan to the rocket launcher, with common to legendary versions, plus grenades, shields and level-up power-ups.

8 leagues

Climb from Rookie to Legend on the Trophy Road. Rivals get sharper as you climb, and tougher leagues pay more coins.

15 heroes

Each with a small perk of their own, and 12 outfits, hats, dances and pistol skins to make them yours.

Planes, chutes & lobbies

Pick from 11 plane designs, 20 parachutes and 7 lobby scenes for the way you drop in.

Plays anywhere

Fully offline, with touch controls, a game pad or your TV remote. Progress stays on your device; no account needed.

Free to play

Crown Drop is free with occasional ads between matches on phones and tablets, and optional ads you can choose to watch to revive, earn coins or unlock a featured item. Gem packs and a one-time Premium upgrade are available in the game; Premium adds 5,000 gems, removes the between-match ads and gives you one free revive every match. The Android TV version has no ads.
