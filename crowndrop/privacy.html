Crown Drop: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

This policy explains how the game Crown Drop: Pocket Royale ("the app"), published by SUMAR_TECH ("we"), handles information. It covers the app on Android phones, tablets and Chromebooks, and on Android TV.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. You don't need an account to play, and we don't ask for your name, email or phone number. Every match is played on your device against computer-controlled rivals; there is no online multiplayer and no chat. Your progress (account level, trophies, coins and gems, heroes, outfits and other items you own, and your settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. We run no servers of our own that receive it.

Advertising

On phones, tablets and Chromebooks the app shows ads through Google AdMob: an occasional full-screen ad when you leave a results screen, and optional ads you can choose to watch, for example to revive in a match, for free coins, or to unlock a featured store item. The Android TV version shows no ads, and players who own Premium see no between-match ads. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or a region with similar privacy laws, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy & ad choices. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (gem packs and the one-time Premium upgrade) are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only receives a confirmation from Google Play that a purchase was made, so it can add the gems or unlock Premium, and so Premium can be restored if you reinstall.

Google Play Games

If the app signs you in to Google Play Games, it may show your Play Games player name and avatar in the game. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, and we do not store it anywhere but your device.

Permissions

The app uses internet access for ads and purchases, and vibration for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, files or location.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Ad and purchase traffic from the app travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
