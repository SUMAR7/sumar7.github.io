Waddle Wings: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how the game Waddle Wings: Duck Adventure ("the app"), published by SUMAR_TECH ("we"), handles information. It covers the app on Android TV and Google TV devices.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information or any other data about you or your device. You don't need an account to play, and we never ask for a name, email address, phone number, age or location.

The game is played entirely offline on your TV. There is no multiplayer, no chat, no leaderboards and no links to websites or social media. Your progress (the levels you have finished, the seeds you have collected, the feathers and hats you own and wear, and your sound and picture settings) is saved only on your device, and is deleted when you uninstall the app. We run no servers that receive it.

Advertising and analytics

The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools of any kind. It does not use the advertising ID.

Purchases

Waddle Wings is bought once on Google Play. That purchase is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details. There are no purchases inside the app: everything in the Duck Shop is paid for with seeds collected by playing.

Permissions

The app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, files or location. It declares Android's standard internet permission because it is built on the system web view, but the game itself sends and receives nothing over the network.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages, including children under 13. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children. It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no way to contact other people.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
