Car Wash Detailing

Repair it. Wash it. Make it shine.

Muddy supercars, lifted 4x4s, police cruisers and huge work trucks roll into your detailing shop. Hammer out the dents, sand away the rust, swap the flat tyres, cover every panel in thick snow foam, blast it clean with the pressure washer and wax it to a mirror finish. Then drive it into the owner’s bay yourself, or hand the keys to the valet.

How to play

  1. Pick a job from the job board. Every car arrives with its own dents, rust, flat tyres and mud.
  2. Choose a tool and rub over the car. Follow the yellow ring to the next spot that needs work. Once most of the job is done, the tool finishes the rest by itself.
  3. Repair, then wash, then polish. Foam loosens the grime so the pressure washer can rinse it away; wax turns dull paint into a mirror.
  4. Park it or valet it. Drive the finished car into the owner’s bay with the wheel, pedals and gears for a parking tip, or let the valet take it.

What’s inside

Real detailing work

Dent hammer, orbital sander, tyre changer, snow foam cannon, pressure washer and wax polish.

Supercars to fire engines

Exotic sports cars, off-road 4x4s, police cruisers, army jeeps, tow trucks and a semi truck.

Grow your shop

Earn cash on every job, buy new tools as the jobs get tougher and upgrade them to work faster.

Indoor parking garage

Steer into tight bays with park-assist sensors, a 3rd-person or top-down camera, and a valet when you’d rather not.

Before & after

Every job ends with a before-and-after photo of the car that you can share with friends.

Plays offline

No account needed. Your cash, tools and progress stay on your device.

Free to play

Car Wash Detailing is free with occasional ads between jobs, never while you work on a car, and optional videos you can choose to watch to double the cash from a job or to get a tool you can’t afford yet. Remove Ads, a one-time purchase, turns off the ads between jobs for good and makes the double cash free after every job.
