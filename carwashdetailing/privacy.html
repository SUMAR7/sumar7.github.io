Car Wash Detailing: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

This policy explains how the game Car Wash Detailing ("the app"), published by SUMAR_TECH ("we"), handles information. It covers the app on Android phones, tablets and Chromebooks.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. You don't need an account to play, and we don't ask for your name, email or phone number. The game is played offline on your device; there is no multiplayer and no chat. Your progress (finished jobs and stars, coins, the tools and upgrades you own, and your settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. We run no servers of our own that receive it.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: an occasional full-screen ad between jobs, never while you work on a car, and optional videos you can choose to watch, for example to double the coins from a job or to get a tool you can't afford yet. Players who own the Remove Ads purchase see no ads between jobs. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or a region with similar privacy laws, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time with the Privacy button on the title screen. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only receives a confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made, so it can remove the ads, and so it can be restored if you reinstall.

Sharing

After a job you can share a before-and-after picture of the car. The picture is made on your device and only goes to the app you pick in your phone’s share menu. We do not receive it.

Permissions

The app uses internet access for ads and purchases. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, files or location.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Ad and purchase traffic from the app travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
